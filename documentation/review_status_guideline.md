# Review Status Guideline

## pending
Imported or newly added data that has not yet been reviewed.

## reviewed
Checked by a reviewer, but not yet approved for model training.

## approved
Confirmed as usable for training, validation, or reporting.

## rejected
Marked as unsuitable due to poor image quality, wrong label, duplicate issue, or irrelevant content.

## needs_revision
Requires correction, relabeling, or additional annotation before approval.
